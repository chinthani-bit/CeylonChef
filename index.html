Culinary Identity

Sri Lankan, energetic, reliable & internationally acclaimed chef.

Chef Mahinda Dharmawardhana has built a global career spanning over 20 years in prestigious kitchens across Sri Lanka, Maldives, Dubai, Macau, and now Finland. He masterfully blends authentic Upcountry Ceylon traditions with Nordic ingredient purity — bold flavors, elegance, and passion on every plate.

As an Executive Chef at Maalaishotelli Morva (Jämsänkoski, Finland) and previously at luxury retreats like Sigiriya Kings Resort, Kalundewa Retreat, and Parisian Macao, Mahinda brings Mediterranean warmth and Asian artistry to Finnish tables.
